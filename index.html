Questions

Everything pros ask us

What's included in my weekly rent?

Everything: your private furnished suite, all utilities, high-speed Wi-Fi, common-area cleaning, security, and 24-hour keyed access so you can work on your own schedule. Weekly rent is all-in, with no extra fees. Call or book a tour for current pricing.

Do you take a cut of my earnings?

Never. You keep 100% of your service revenue, tips, and product sales. Your only cost is your flat weekly rent.

What do I need to lease a suite?

An active South Carolina license for your profession — cosmetology, barbering, esthetics, nails, massage, tattoo, nutrition, personal training, and more — plus liability insurance (we can point you to affordable providers). Most pros are approved within 48 hours.

Can I sell my own retail products?

Yes — and you keep every dollar. Each suite includes retail shelving, and Medium and Large Studios include a full retail wall.

How long is the lease?

We offer 6-month and 12-month leases. A refundable deposit (typically two weeks of rent) holds your suite, and rent can be paid weekly through our online portal.

Can I customize my suite?

Please do — custom suite designs are our specialty. Décor, signage, your own music, your own layout: your suite should look and feel like your brand. Pre-leasing with custom build-outs is available now.
